de esta carpeta. El sobre y sus solapas son CSS; el sello y el fondo floral
son imagenes. Los destinatarios siguen siendo texto personalizado.

## Prompt del sello

Referencia: `sello2.jpeg`. Fondo transparente solicitado.

```text
Use case: background-extraction and precise-object-edit. Edit this gold wax seal into a production-ready isolated website asset on a genuinely transparent background. Remove the entire checkerboard background (it is baked into the source), all shadows outside the seal and all surrounding margins; retain a subtle short contact shadow only directly at the seal edge if needed. Keep the antique champagne-gold wax material, round recessed impression, fine surface grain, smooth organically uneven thick melted rim, realistic metallic highlights, and frontal overhead angle. Add elegant engraved intertwined serif initials J and L separated by a small ampersand: exact text 'J & L'. Make the initials look stamped INTO the wax, matching gold color with natural relief shadows, not black printed lettering. Center one complete seal filling 85% of a square canvas; no perspective tilt, no other objects, no background, no decorative lettering outside the monogram, no spikes or sawtooth edges. This must look like a real gold sealing-wax stamp for an elegant chocolate-brown wedding envelope. Save final PNG asset for project use.
```

## Prompt del fondo

Imagen nueva; la paleta y la composicion siguen `img-sobre.jpeg`.

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: elegant vintage wedding invitation full-screen background, wide landscape composition, approx 3:2. Create only an ivory handmade laid-paper background with very delicate visible paper fibers, softly aged cream warm off-white, no dark vignette. Paint two sparse botanical arrangements in loose old botanical watercolor: one entering from the upper-left corner and one entering from the lower-right corner. Small soft muted dusty mauve / muted wine-brown garden flowers and sage / olive-green leaves, translucent low-contrast pigment, exceptionally refined and restrained. Botanical clusters should occupy only the outer 15-20% of the image, more at corners, do not form a continuous border. The middle 70% must be completely empty light ivory paper so an HTML envelope can be placed on top. Flat overhead view, tactile stationery, balanced graceful diagonals, romantic antique correspondence aesthetic. NO envelope, NO wax stamp, NO seal, NO lettering, NO text, NO monogram, NO frame, NO objects, NO interface, NO gold splashes. Use an elegant matte texture rather than shiny or synthetic surface. This is a background ASSET, not a screenshot or mockup.
```
